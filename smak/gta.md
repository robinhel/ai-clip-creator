# GTA clips that spread

- Prefer calm, funny, shareable moments over hype: NPCs, characters, absurd situations, funny fails, a quotable line. Test: would someone send this to a friend?
- The moment matters more than skill or excitement; a quiet funny line can beat a loud clutch.
- One moment per clip. Start right before the line or action. End right after the payoff or the laugh; the shortest clip the run allows is fine.
- Headline: when a spoken line carries the moment, make the headline that line as a short quote in quotation marks, copied from the transcript (max 8 words and 40 characters including the quotation marks). Otherwise a short honest opinion. No invented facts.
